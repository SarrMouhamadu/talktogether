Privacy Policy for WaxUp

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Welcome to WaxUp, operated by Sylla English Academy ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your personal data and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application WaxUp (the "Application").

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that you provide directly to us when using WaxUp:

  • Account Information: When you register or sign in, we collect your full name, username, email address, country of residence, and self-assessed English level.
  • Audio and Voice Content: When you participate in live voice debate rooms or record audio notes for the community feed, your voice is transmitted in real time or recorded as an audio snippet. We do not sell your voice recordings to third parties.
  • User-Generated Content: Any topics, posts, text comments, debate votes, or bookmarks you share on the platform.
  • Photos: If you choose to attach an image to a community feed post, we access the photo you explicitly select. We do not access your full photo library without permission.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • To operate, maintain, and provide live voice debate rooms and community features.
  • To authenticate your account and ensure secure access.
  • To manage speaking turns, debate queues, and calculate community practice streaks.
  • To enforce our Zero-Tolerance Community Guidelines and protect our community from hate speech, harassment, inappropriate content, and spam.
  • To respond to your feedback, suggestions, or customer support requests.

3. Device Permissions

WaxUp requires specific system permissions to function properly:

  • Microphone (NSMicrophoneUsageDescription): Required strictly when you speak in live voice debates or record audio posts. We do not record or access audio in the background without active room participation.
  • Photo Library: Requested only when you choose to attach a photo to a community post.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We respect your privacy and do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data. We only share information in the following limited circumstances:

  • Public Community Profile: Your username, initials, country, and English level are visible to other users in live rooms and on the community feed.
  • Safety & Legal Compliance: We may disclose information if required to comply with legal obligations, enforce community safety rules, or protect the rights and safety of our users.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your personal data for as long as your account remains active. You have full control over your data:

  • You can edit your profile information at any time directly inside the Application.
  • You can permanently delete your account and associated local data at any time in Profile > Delete Account. Upon deletion, your account and private profile are irreversibly removed.

6. Children's Privacy

WaxUp is designed for general audiences and language learners. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided personal data, we will immediately delete such information.

7. Zero-Tolerance Safety & Content Moderation

WaxUp enforces a strict zero-tolerance policy against hate speech, harassment, nudity, and violence. Users can report any objectionable content or user via the in-app reporting system. Reported accounts and content are reviewed promptly, and violations result in immediate content removal and permanent suspension.
